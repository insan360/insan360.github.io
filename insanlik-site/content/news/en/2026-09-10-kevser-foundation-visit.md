As İnsanlık Eli Humanitarian Aid Association, we visited the Kevser Foundation and its President, Ferhat Çalış, and held a comprehensive consultation on humanitarian aid activities, needs in the field, and potential joint initiatives that could be carried out in the future.

During this meaningful meeting, we discussed ways to reach people in need in a stronger and more sustainable manner. We hope that this gathering will lead to lasting good and positive outcomes
 We would like to thank the Kevser Foundation for their kind hospitality and valuable insights.
