As part of its ongoing humanitarian response in the Gaza Strip, Humanity Hand Association for Relief and Solidarity implemented a project to provide safe drinking water to displaced and conflict-affected families in [Area/Camp Name].

 The project was launched in response to the severe shortage of safe drinking water caused by damage to water networks and limited access to clean water sources.

 Approximately [ 500] litres of drinking water were distributed to [ ] families, benefiting around [ ] individuals, including children, women, older persons, people with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

 Water was transported and distributed through [water tankers / storage tanks / bottled water], following a field distribution plan designed to ensure access for displacement sites and communities experiencing interrupted water supplies.

 Field teams coordinated closely with local community representatives to organize the distribution process, verify beneficiary needs, and prioritize families without alternative access to safe water.

 The project aims to reduce health risks associated with contaminated water while helping displaced families meet their daily needs for drinking, cooking, and personal hygiene.

 Providing safe water remains one of the Association's highest humanitarian priorities in the Gaza Strip because of its direct impact on public health, dignity, and disease prevention.

 Humanity Hand Association remains committed to expanding its water and sanitation programmes, increasing water distribution, and extending services to additional communities based on available resources and identified humanitarian needs.
