Construction is underway on the mosque in Benin being built in memory of the late Hacı Ahmed.

Upon completion, the mosque—featuring a 200-square-meter prayer area—will serve as a center where approximately 350 to 400 people can worship simultaneously.

We plan to open the mosque for public use as soon as possible, establishing it as a gathering place for the local community and a center where children can receive Quranic education.
