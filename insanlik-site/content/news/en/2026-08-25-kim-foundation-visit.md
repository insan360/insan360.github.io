Dr. Adem Varıcı, Vice Chairman of the Board of the **Center for Cross-cultural Communication (KİM) Foundation**, visited **İnsanlık Eli Association**.

During the visit, both organizations exchanged information and views on their ongoing activities, current areas of operation, active projects, and field experience.

The meeting also included discussions on potential areas of future cooperation and joint projects, with particular focus on **cross-cultural communication, humanitarian aid, education, and social support**.

We sincerely thank **Dr. Adem Varıcı** and the **Center for Cross-cultural Communication (KİM) Foundation** for their kind visit and thoughtful gift, and we look forward to strengthening and expanding the cooperation between our two organizations.
