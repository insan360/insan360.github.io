During the visit by Bilal Vardi, General Coordinator of the Nezir Association, and his team to our association, discussions were held regarding the corporate infrastructure and transformation initiatives carried out by the Information Technologies Unit, as well as processes related to legislation and compliance.

The meetings also provided an opportunity to exchange information and ideas concerning the humanitarian aid activities conducted by both organizations, their operational regions, ongoing and planned future projects, and field experiences.

We extend our thanks to the Nezir Association for their kind visit.
