The Food Security Programme focuses on combating severe hunger and guaranteeing consistent access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food for vulnerable and crisis-stricken families. Through a multi-faceted approach combining direct food parcel delivery, daily hot meal kitchens, fresh produce, and infant nutritional support, our interventions mitigate malnutrition and enhance long-term community food stability in compliance with global humanitarian standards.
