- Construct and rehabilitate dignified sanitation facilities and emergency latrines.
- Drastically lower the risk of waterborne diseases and public health outbreaks.
- Promote hygienic practices at both household and community levels.
- Restore safe and dignified living conditions for camp and displacement populations.

Access to clean water and adequate sanitation is a basic human right. The WASH Programme provides sustainable water solutions, sanitary infrastructure, and health awareness campaigns to emergency camps and vulnerable neighborhoods. By constructing water wells, rehabilitating latrines, distributing hygiene kits, and conducting public health drives, we prevent waterborne epidemics and restore human dignity.
