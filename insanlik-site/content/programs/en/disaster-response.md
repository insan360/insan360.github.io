- Reduce the severe social and economic long-term impacts of disasters.
- Build back safer and strengthen community preparedness for future crises.

The Disaster Response and Early Recovery Programme bridges the vital gap between immediate emergency relief and long-term sustainable development. We assist affected populations in rebuilding their lives through rubble removal, housing rehabilitation, restoration of basic public infrastructure (schools, water networks), and recovery of community-level services.
