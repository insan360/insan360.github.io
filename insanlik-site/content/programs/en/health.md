goals:
- Improve direct access to primary, specialized, and emergency medical services.
- Supply critical medical equipment, pharmaceuticals, and consumables to frontline hospitals.
- Reduce preventable mortality and morbidity rates among crisis-affected populations.
- Deliver maternal, newborn, child healthcare, and mental health/psychosocial support.
- Promote preventative healthcare awareness and public health education.

The Health Programme saves lives and reinforces fragile health systems by expanding access to essential primary and specialized healthcare. We equip overburdened hospitals, run mobile medical clinics, provide essential pharmaceuticals, and offer maternal-child health services along with Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) to conflict and disaster survivors.
