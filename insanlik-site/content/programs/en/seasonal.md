Seasonal Programmes address the predictable surge in humanitarian needs tied to climatic winter harshness, Ramadan, Udhiya (Qurbani), and Back-to-School seasons. Through targeted relief campaigns—including winter heating fuel and blankets, Ramadan food parcels, community Iftar meals, Qurbani meat distribution, and Eid gifts—we alleviate economic stress and uphold social solidarity.
