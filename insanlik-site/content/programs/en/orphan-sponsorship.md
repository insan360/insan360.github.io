goals:
- Provide continuous monthly financial stipends to support orphans and widowed mothers.
- Ensure full access to quality education, school supplies, and healthcare services.
- Protect children's emotional well-being through targeted psychosocial and recreational support.
- Improve living conditions and family stability for households that lost their breadwinner.
- Create a secure, safe, and nurturing environment for child development.

The Orphan Sponsorship Programme provides holistic, long-term support for orphaned children and their caregiver families. By delivering monthly financial stipends, educational materials, healthcare coverage, and psychosocial recreational activities, we empower orphaned children to overcome hardship and build a secure, promising future with dignity.
