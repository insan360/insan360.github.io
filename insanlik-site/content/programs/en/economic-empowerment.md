True humanitarian aid builds lasting independence. The Economic Empowerment and Livelihoods Programme equips low-income families, youth, and women with productive assets, small business grants, and vocational training. By transitioning beneficiaries from aid dependency to economic self-sufficiency, we foster local market recovery and long-term community resilience.
