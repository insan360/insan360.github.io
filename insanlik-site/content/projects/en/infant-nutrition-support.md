title: Infant Nutrition Support
program: food-security
place: Gaza Palestine
image: /img/projects/infant-nutrition-support.jpg
objectives:
- Provide infant formula to vulnerable families with babies.
- Support safe and adequate infant nutrition where appropriate.
- Reduce the financial burden on displaced and crisis-affected households.
- Prioritize families facing severe shortages and limited access to essential supplies.
impact: Your support helps a vulnerable family secure essential nutrition for a baby during a period of severe scarcity, easing pressure on caregivers and protecting the child's daily nutritional needs.
---

The ongoing humanitarian crisis has made infant formula difficult to access and increasingly unaffordable for many families.

This project provides appropriate infant nutrition supplies to vulnerable households with babies, helping caregivers meet one of their children's most essential daily needs.
