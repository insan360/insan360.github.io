- Provide essential food supplies to vulnerable households.
- Help families meet immediate nutritional needs.
- Prioritize displaced and highly vulnerable families.
- Reduce pressure on household resources during periods of acute food insecurity.
impact: Your support places essential food directly in the hands of families in need, helping them meet urgent household needs and protect limited resources for other necessities.

Rising food insecurity and disrupted access to essential goods have placed significant pressure on families across Gaza.

This project provides food parcels and staple supplies to vulnerable households, helping them cover urgent food needs during the humanitarian crisis.
